Modified for Rave Board: selected the six Kilter Original layouts, converted
placement IDs to LED positions, and added board coordinates, hold-set IDs, artwork
dimensions and provenance. Polygon offsets remain as supplied upstream. The
conversion script lives at scripts/import-hold-shapes.mjs
in the Rave Board source. No Boardsesh runtime or source raster images are bundled.

LED/placement mapping and source-art dimensions use Grip Connect:

Boardsesh traced these silhouettes from Kilter/Aurora board artwork. These are
image-derived shapes, not factory CAD, physical measurements or calibrated LED
rim boundaries. The displayed glow is approximate. The 12 x 14 artwork lacks
51 shapes, which are explicitly shown as approximate rings.

The repository licenses do not establish a separate permission grant or official
endorsement from Kilter/Aurora for the underlying artwork. Rave Board makes
no claim of manufacturer affiliation, ownership of that artwork or measured
optical accuracy.
